# Gwen Skedaddle

The native 147219 flyout protocol preserves all 25 selectors listed in the
supplied protocol specification, including selector 13 for CLEANSED. The codec
also preserves unknown selectors and decrypted transport padding.

Eight observed ARAL casts from an enemy Gwen viewpoint contained a targeted
ability-use notice with selector 2 and the no-target sentinel, the self buff
806, CLEANSED, and the active movement buff 813. Each observed Gwen had equipped
slot 0 at level 1. The subsequent cooldown setter identifies
`Ability__Gwen__A` and sets it to zero. The supplemental ability and talent
definitions identify the buffs as the active B effects and assign the A reset
to the rare Speed Loader talent. The separate Skedaddling talent has different
effects.

An authoritative learned B cast consumes energy, starts its own cooldown,
removes modeled movement impairments, grants half a second of immunity to those
impairments, and grants the configured movement speed for 2.2 seconds.
The A reset requires a named Speed Loader grant. In ARAL the reserved,
home-authorized level-one slot-zero equip can establish that grant for Gwen.
The output sequence is sent to enemies that can see the already declared actor
and to observers with its declaration. The original scope for the caster and
allies, native inbound Gwen learn/cast selectors, passive movement speed, and
Gwen's A damage remain unverified or unimplemented.
